Cadastral plan of the territory
===============================

The tool forms list of ID of cadastral units.

Input:

* Area of interest - polygon in GeoJSON format. Area must be up to 50 km².
* Mode - choose between kpt and kvzu.

Output:

* CSV file

Launch the tool: https://toolbox.nextgis.com/t/egrn_kvartals_cover

**Try the tool in action**

1. Click on the **Demo** button above the tool form. The fields are filled in with demo values.
2. Click on the **Run** button.
